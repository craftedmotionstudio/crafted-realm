# Keep v2 tower access candidate

Actual Blender source and exports, unpublished. 7214 triangles, 30 primitives.

Both towers have stacked 1.25 m cardinal switchback flights, 1.2 m deep landings, open floor apertures and lookout parapets. The east tower expansion is recorded in the contract. 80 tower treads plus the preserved 16 hall treads were measured from actual mesh vertices. 396 vertical scene rays and 120 doorway crossing rays and 280 horizontal stair-entry rays passed; these are discrete geometry samples, not runtime avatar validation.

Full-world placement, navigation/player integration, animation, reference review and Safe Publish remain unimplemented. The east wallwalk still has its closed north stop; turret entry is on the ground.
