Actual Blender source and exports, unpublished. 7222 triangles, 30 primitives.

Both towers have stacked 1.25 m cardinal switchback flights, 1.2 m deep landings, open floor apertures and lookout parapets. The east tower expansion to center(10,7), apothem3 is recorded in the contract. Full-world placement, navigation/player integration, animation, reference review and Safe Publish remain unimplemented. The east wallwalk still has its closed north stop; turret entry is on the ground.
