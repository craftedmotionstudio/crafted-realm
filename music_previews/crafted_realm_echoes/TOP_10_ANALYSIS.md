# Echoes of the Old Roads — analysis and original counterparts

There is no official, objective OSRS “Top 10.” This collection uses a consensus set: tracks that recur in community favorite/nostalgia discussions, overlap with a published ranking, or represent the official classic soundtrack’s most recognizable early-game identities.

Sources consulted:

- [Community favorite-track discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/2007scape/comments/10d9r36/what_is_your_favorite_osrs_music_track/)
- [Community nostalgia discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/2007scape/comments/1g14dnr/what_are_your_favorite_and_most_nostalgic_osrs/)
- [Published top-ten ranking](https://www.tonestart.com/best-runescape-songs/)
- [RuneScape: Original Soundtrack Classics track list](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/User%3AIN008/Complete_Projects/Soundtrack)

## Collection specification

- 10 original compositions, 68 bars each
- 20,064 authored MIDI note events total
- All 10 sampled game instruments appear in every piece
- 16-note peak polyphony in every piece
- Individual MP3 and editable multitrack MIDI files
- Approximately 24 minutes 36 seconds of music
- One continuous full-album MP3 plus a ZIP containing the complete collection
