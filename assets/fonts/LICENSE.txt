Realm Small (realm_small.ttf, realm_small_bold.ttf)
==================================================

Realm Small is Crafted Realm's own bitmap-look interface font. Every glyph was drawn pixel by pixel for this
project in tools/build_pixel_font.py (12 px em: 8 px capitals, 6 px x-height, 3 px descenders, 1 px strokes; the
bold cut doubles each stem one pixel to the right) and compiled to TrueType with fontTools. No third-party font
data, outlines or bitmaps were used, copied or traced (in particular nothing from Jagex / RuneScape fonts).

Copyright (c) 2026 Crafted Motion. Made for, and free to use in, Crafted Realm.

The owner may publish the font for others under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org)
by adding the OFL text here; until then it ships only as part of the game.

Rebuild: python tools/build_pixel_font.py   (writes both .ttf files and scratchpad/holm_ui_v3/font_specimen.png)
Use at font-size 12px (or 24px) so one glyph pixel lands on one screen pixel.
