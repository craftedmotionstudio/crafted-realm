## Character/NPC assets visible across the references

These are genuine potential assets, but they are not static environment objects and therefore must follow the project's modeled-NPC production gate:

- Larissa-equivalent lighthouse guide/keeper character.
- Humanoid cave dweller family visible on several ledges.
- Large aquatic/reptilian cave predator family visible on the lower floor.
- Smaller/alternate cave predator silhouettes visible around the pool and rear shelf.

They require separate concept approval, custom Blender topology, animations, combat/readability work, and gameplay data. They are recorded—not silently omitted—but remain outside this environmental asset revision under the standing NPC-last rule.

## Acceptance rule

No lighthouse comparison can be described as “all assets covered” unless every row above is either:

1. visibly modeled and integrated;
2. supplied by an identified existing landscape/UI system;
3. an explicit owner-approved replacement such as ladders for the spiral; or
4. named as a future modeled character/NPC deliverable rather than disguised as scenery.
