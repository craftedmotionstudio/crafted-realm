# Tutor's Holm compact flow and Workyard U6 start

Date: 2026-07-18

## Outcome

Tutor's Holm now has a compact, shippable graduation route without deleting its richer optional places. The live
curriculum contains 13 required successful actions across Guide Hall, Survival Workyard, Mine Gatehouse/Training
Cavern, Warden's Ridge banking, and Lastlight. Five deeper lessons remain optional. The target first completion is
20–30 minutes; players may remain on the island and explore as long as they wish.

The Guide Hall relief chart is now the first real gate. Pulling Lastlight's beacon lever is the final real gate.
The ferry stays locked until that sequence is complete.

## Compatibility

Saves now record a stable tutorial lesson id in addition to the curriculum revision and numeric step. Revision-4
incomplete saves migrate by lesson meaning. A player formerly on the mandatory bread step moves to the cavern
descent rather than being assigned an unrelated new index.
