## Recurring failure pattern and practical response

The central pattern is visible in the owner's own words: a functional or technically exported prototype is repeatedly presented while its footprint, furnishing, surface detail or scale still looks unfinished. The owner then restates the same requirement in progressively more concrete terms. This is a synthesis of the quoted messages, not an assertion about the quality of every historical pass.

1. **Compose each building around its use and silhouette.** Choose genuinely different connected plans: an L-shaped domestic wing, a U-shaped courtyard compound, a long workshop with an offset service wing, a bank with a projecting bay, a tower joined to a lower occupied wing, and a fortified court with unequal towers. These are proposed responses to the evidence, not a claim that those exact designs have been approved or built. Retain a few compact cottages for contrast.
2. **Make projections usable.** Wings, turrets and courts must produce believable rooms, entrances, stairs, service access or sheltered work areas. Cosmetic cubes attached to a square shell repeat the rejected result.
3. **Treat the environment as authored art.** Match the references' scale of stone courses, wood grain or plank breaks, plaster variation, turf/path edges, shore layers, cave recesses and planted density. Use original textures/materials to reproduce the visual language; do not copy proprietary image pixels into game assets. Parent's direct image review must decide the actual material implementation.
4. **Furnish and animate from an inventory.** Identify every visible prop, its purpose, readable silhouette, source asset, material family and motion/interaction. Verify at gameplay camera distance so detail earns its cost.
5. **Prove ordinary play as well as renders.** Walk through the real door, traverse the added wing, climb the upper floor, use the furnishing/resource and save/reload. Prior reports of teleported elevation and ambiguous ladders explain why geometric existence alone is weak evidence.
6. **Keep acceptance honest.** Separate designed source, Studio review, integrated appearance and verified gameplay. Today's owner rejection supersedes older positive scores. The active objective remains the full tutorial, including all original lessons, Lastlight and departure.
