# Tutor's Holm landscape v1

Date: 2026-07-13  
Status: Phase-2 environment blockout; not final content or building acceptance

## Locked composition

- Authored envelope: 112x96 tiles; 195 catalog chunks; 49 nearby chunks maximum resident.
- Primary journey: 228 cardinal tiles from Arrival Cove through six distinct surface districts to Departure Dock.
- Districts: Arrival Cove, Survival Wood, Lesson Green, Quarry Rise, Warden's Ridge, Mage Headland.
- Eight functional building reservations: guide hall, survival shelter, quest lodge, teaching kitchen,
  mine gatehouse, bank, combat hall, and mage tower.
- Water structure: carved arrival and departure coves, large freshwater pond, long tidal inlet, Tidebridge,
  and a walkable ferry pier with the boat deliberately separated from the spawn beach.
- Underground reservation: 48x40 tiles for the later mining/smithing cavern.
- NPCs and final tutorial services remain deferred until the environment and authoring contract are stable.
