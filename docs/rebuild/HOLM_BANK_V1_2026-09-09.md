# Holm Bank v1 — functional building slice

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: live functional graybox; owner fully-designed art gate still open

## Delivered boundary

Warden's Ridge's Holm Bank reservation (pad `holm_bank`, 10x8 at 157,116) is replaced by the sixth complete
Blender-built Tutor's Holm building, the second on the **required** route: the `open_bank` step now happens inside a
real bank. The vault chest that carried the lesson on the open pad moved into the vault at (159.5,113.5) and still
opens the bank through the engine's own chest path; two teller booths on the counter open the same bank interface,
so the lesson can be earned at the counter the way OSRS teaches it. The Lesson Green/Warden's Ridge road corner is
the front door; the east staff door continues toward the Combat Hall.

## Composition

- **Plan:** gabled banking hall (10x8, 4.4-tile walls, ridge east–west) with a counting-house dormer over the door
  and a stone vault chimney at the north-east corner; a half-octagonal waiting bay on the west wall. A walnut
  teller counter under an iron grille divides the chequered-marble customer hall (south) from the flagstone vault
  (north), with a staff gap at the counter's east end.
- **Doors:** south bank door under a gilded coin sign (opening on tile centre 157.5,120) and east staff door
  (opening on tile centre 162,118.5); both fitted full-frame leaves with over-door infill.
- **Hero and support objects:** two brass-grilled booths with velvet coin trays and lamps; ledger desk with
  scales, ledger, inkwell, quill and coin stacks; founders' plaque; waiting bench; strongbox shelf with nine iron
  boxes; queue posts and velvet rope; coin sacks; key board; wall lantern; rates notice.
