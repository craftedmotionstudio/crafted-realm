# Survival Workyard v2 — compound and furnishing brief

Date: 2026-07-14  
Status: owner-directed redesign; v1 remains live until v2 clears the full gate

## Owner correction

The v1 material and construction aesthetic is useful, but the complete roof silhouette still reads as a
rectangle. That is not accepted final architecture. Important Crafted Realm buildings must be memorable from
their footprint and room program before trim, roof tiles, or furniture are added.

## Purpose

This is the Survival Wood training compound. It is not a magic or ranged classroom. Its release functions are:

- prepare and maintain Woodcutting, Firemaking, Fishing, and Cooking tools;
- teach controlled fire and camp-food handling;
- store storm reserves below ground;
- maintain a covered work court beside the pond;
- reserve a fenced chicken-training socket for the later modelled-NPC phase.

Magic belongs in Mage Tower. Ranged practice belongs in the Combat Hall/training-cavern sequence. This keeps
each building legible instead of turning every room into a mixed tutorial warehouse.

## v2 shape contract

- The top-down footprint must not fit visually inside one simple rectangular reading.
- Use at least three purposeful masses: lodge, connector/crossing, and polygonal hearth/storage room.
- Use at least three roof responses: dominant lodge gable, lower connector/yard cover, polygonal hearth cap.
- Preserve a cardinal, one-tile-wide route from trail entrance through lodge to pond exit.
- The basement ladder must be visible from the gameplay camera with the roof hidden.
- The chicken pen is outdoors or under a yard cover, never implausibly sealed in the food cellar.
- The cellar is a real authored sublevel: shelves, barrels, sacks, preserved food, spare tools, lamp niches,
  and an emergency bunk/bench. It receives a return ladder and cannot trap the player.

## Furnishing contract

Finished rooms use Blender-authored components, not undressed primitive placeholders. The first reusable family
must include an occupational table, stools, shelving, crockery cabinet, rug, fireplace/hearth, ladder, barrels,
sacks, storage chest, wall painting/frame, chicken pen, feed trough, and roost. Each component exposes material
and proportion variants so oak, willow, mahogany, regional paint, wear, and contents change without cloning a
whole room.

The long-term target can exceed one hundred furniture variants, but production scales by combining authored
silhouette families, wood/material families, wear states, and contents. The immediate v2 building must prove
the system with a smaller high-quality set rather than generating one hundred low-value copies.

Anything that moves in reality is animated: fire flicker is mandatory; the chicken remains a deferred modelled
NPC/spawn socket until the environment foundation is accepted.

## Massing studies

1. **Hearth Court** — offset lodge connected to an eight-sided teaching hearth; the gap becomes a covered
   work court and chicken pen. Recommended because every unusual mass has a survival function.
2. **Crooked Larder** — deep L-shaped lodge with a clipped storm-store bay and transverse work wing.
3. **Rain-Catch U** — unequal U-shaped wings around a working court and water trough, with a polygonal end room.

Only the selected footprint receives production walls, fitted roofs, furnishings, cellar, semantics, collision,
Studio integration, and live-game replacement.
