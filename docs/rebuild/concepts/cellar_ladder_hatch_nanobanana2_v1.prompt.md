# Storm cellar ladder and descent — Nano Banana 2 prompt v1

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production reference for a custom-topology Blender traversal prop.
Create an original low-poly 3D concept sheet for a cozy medieval browser RPG, similar in broad art direction
to Old School RuneScape's readable, hand-shaped early-2000s low-poly charm. Do not copy any existing game's
ladder, hatch, composition, textures or protected asset data.

Show the same traversal family from front three-quarter, side and elevated gameplay-camera views:

- a crooked but structurally believable aged-oak ladder with two hand-hewn rails;
- six or seven individually shaped rungs with consistent human climbing spacing;
- simple forged-iron rail braces and visible wooden joinery;
- a raised timber-and-masonry cellar sill fitted around the opening;
- a clearly descending dark opening with visible inner wall thickness and two lower rung silhouettes;
- no grass, flat black decal, modern hardware, trapdoor floating in space or pristine machine-cut lumber.

Scale is mandatory: include a neutral 1.9-unit low-poly medieval character standing beside the ladder and a
one-unit square floor grid. The ladder should rise roughly 2.3–2.5 units, provide about 0.6 units of clear rung
width, and lean naturally against the sill. Use warm flat-shaded aged oak, soot-dark iron and irregular gray-tan
masonry. Neutral studio background, soft shadows, no text, no UI, no dramatic perspective and no photorealism.
