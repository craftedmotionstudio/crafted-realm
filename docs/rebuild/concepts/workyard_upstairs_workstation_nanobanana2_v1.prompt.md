# Workyard upstairs workstation v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the Survival Lodge working/storage family

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a purposeful medieval survival-lodge workstation family. Show five clearly separated, fully designed objects: a broad irregular oak tool-maintenance bench, a short three-legged work stool, a fitted crockery hutch, a small wall-mounted mug shelf, and an empty stave bucket. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin and a faint one-tile floor grid only as scale references. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view.

The workbench must not be a plain rectangle: three uneven edge planks form the top, two visibly pegged A-frame trestles support it, a low stretcher carries a small shelf, and one front corner is clipped. Arrange a modeled bronze hatchet, faceted grey whetstone, three splitting wedges, a wooden mallet, and a small pitch pot in fitted recesses so every object has an obvious maintenance purpose and nothing floats.

The hutch must have a shaped stepped crown, tapered side posts, two lower doors, three open shelves, plate grooves, and player-scale blue-and-cream earthenware. The separate mug shelf is a short scalloped wall shelf with four chunky handled mugs hanging from wooden pegs. The bucket uses individual tapered oak staves, two dark iron hoops, a visible warm-wood interior, a stout arched handle, and a small pouring notch. The stool should look repaired and practical rather than decorative.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped silhouettes, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm aged oak, darker endgrain, charcoal iron, muted brass, cream and dusty-blue pottery. Construction and wear must come from topology and restrained face-color variation. Cozy, humble, medieval, workmanlike, slightly whimsical, and readable from an elevated game camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no room walls, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, photorealism, dense beveling, smooth modern furniture, ornate luxury carving, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, generic box tables, floating tools, oversized furniture, paper-thin shelves, and objects intersecting each other.
