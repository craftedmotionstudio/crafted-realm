# Cellar quiet-corner v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2  
**Use case:** stylized-concept  
**Purpose:** original concept reference for a coordinated custom-topology Blender furnishing family

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean 3D low-poly concept render of a cozy medieval cellar seating vignette made from four clearly readable, fully designed objects: two genuinely different chairs, one small side table, and one yarn basket. Arrange them as a believable conversational corner with no walls or room geometry: a tall hand-hewn oak fireside chair on the left angled inward, a lower repaired spindle work chair on the right angled inward, an irregular small table between them, and the yarn basket tucked beside the lower chair. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view, while keeping every construction detail visible enough to rebuild in Blender.

The tall chair must have a memorable asymmetric silhouette: gently raked high back, two crooked uprights ending in simple faceted finials, a broad shaped crest rail with one original shallow crescent notch, three uneven back slats, thick polygonal arms, an irregular wide plank seat, splayed six-sided legs, and visible mortise-and-peg construction. Add a folded muted-russet wool shawl over one arm as a separate readable shape.

The lower work chair must not be a scaled copy: use a shorter curved spindle back, five chunky hand-cut spindles, a woven ochre rush seat, slimmer splayed legs, a low side stretcher, and one obvious dark-iron repair strap around a rear upright. Give it a practical, well-used silhouette.

The table must avoid a square box: use a thick irregular seven-sided oak top, three splayed legs, triangular lower stretchers, peg joints, and one small faceted earthenware cup on top. The handled willow basket should have an oval woven body, stout arched handle, three large faceted wool balls in muted rust, moss green, and undyed cream, two short wooden knitting needles, and one short trailing yarn strand that remains thick and readable.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped custom silhouettes; large readable polygon planes; controlled flat or vertex-color-like shading; warm aged oak, charcoal iron, honey rush, muted russet, moss, and cream; restrained edge wear expressed through a few darker and lighter face colors rather than painted texture. Cozy, humble, practical, medieval, and slightly whimsical. All objects must read clearly at Crafted Realm's elevated gameplay camera.

Background and presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadow only, generous padding, no floor tiles, no walls, no characters, no text, no labels, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, smooth modern furniture, dense bevels, realistic PBR textures, photorealism, ornate luxury carving, Minecraft cubes, Roblox proportions, toy-plastic surfaces, duplicated chair geometry, floating parts, excessively thin spindles, and clutter that hides construction.
