# Cellar wall torch v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create one original medieval wall-mounted torch concept for Crafted Realm's underground storm cellar. The complete object must be visible: a slightly crooked hand-hewn ashwood torch shaft angled upward from a fitted forged-iron wall bracket; an irregular diamond-shaped hammered backplate with two large rivets; a curved supporting hook and lower brace; a shallow blackened iron soot/drip cup beneath the fire; thick overlapping pitch-soaked cloth wraps around the torch head; and a large three-layer faceted flame with an ember-red outer base, orange middle, and small pale-gold core. Add controlled asymmetry, purposeful dents, worn iron edges, charred wood near the flame, and visibly fitted joins. The silhouette must read instantly from an elevated isometric gameplay camera.

Use large readable polygon planes, deliberate hand-shaped topology, flat or vertex-color-like shading, a small warm palette, minimal surface texture, and cozy 2007-era fantasy proportions. Show the torch mounted against a small plain neutral stone-wall sample so the bracket orientation is unambiguous. Three-quarter elevated product/reference view, full object centered with generous margin.

No glass lantern cage. No modern lantern. No glossy mobile-game finish, dense beveling, photoreal PBR, Minecraft or Roblox block language, tiny micro-detail, text, logo, watermark, character, room clutter, or copied proprietary design.
