# Workyard exterior U3 timber rack v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the Survival Workyard exterior timber-storage family (U3.02)

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a medieval woodcutter's outdoor timber-storage rack, fully loaded. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin and a faint one-tile floor grid only as scale references. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view.

The rack must not be a generic shelf: two slightly leaning hand-hewn end frames of pegged upright posts with diagonal cross-braces stand on flat foot stones, joined by two low bearer rails that keep the firewood off the ground. Stacked on the bearers lie roughly ten individually cut logs in two to three nesting rows — every log a different length, thickness, and slight tilt, each with a bright fresh-cut endgrain face and darker weathered bark. One log is noticeably shorter, leaving an honest gap in the stack. Two visible hemp ropes wrap over the whole load and around the bearer rails, pulled tight with a simple knot and a short dangling end, clearly restraining the logs.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped silhouettes, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm aged oak, pale fresh-cut endgrain, darker bark, charcoal iron pegs, honey hemp rope. Construction and wear must come from topology and restrained face-color variation. Cozy, humble, medieval, workmanlike, slightly whimsical, and readable from an elevated game camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no room walls, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, photorealism, dense beveling, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, identical cloned cylinder logs, perfectly symmetric frames, floating logs, ropes that touch nothing, and oversized furniture.
