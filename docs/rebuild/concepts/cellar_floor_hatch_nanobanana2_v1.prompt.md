# Workyard cellar floor hatch — Nano Banana 2 corrective concept

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production reference for a custom-topology Blender architectural prop.
Primary request: Design an original compact medieval floor hatch for the timber floor of a cozy browser RPG.
Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an
original design and not copying any exact asset, composition, texture, or protected game data.

Show one consistent hatch in three views: elevated gameplay camera, close three-quarter construction view, and
side cutaway. The hatch must read as part of the floor architecture, not as a freestanding object.

Required design:
- a rectangular opening cut directly into broad aged-oak floorboards, approximately 0.85 by 1.05 character-scale tiles;
- a thin, irregular timber frame fitted flush into the surrounding planks, never a bright stone platform;
- a heavy six-plank wooden hatch lid hinged open and resting low behind the opening, with two restrained dark iron straps;
- a genuinely recessed, near-black shaft with visible inner timber thickness and two or three ladder rungs descending;
- only the tops of two simple wooden ladder rails rise slightly above the floor, no tall ladder or gateway;
- worn corners, uneven plank widths, hand-cut joinery and large readable polygon planes;
- a neutral 1.9-unit low-poly medieval character beside it for scale, with the opening no wider than the character's arm span.

Style/medium: warm flat-shaded low-poly 3D, deliberately hand-shaped silhouette, early-2000s fantasy-game readability,
minimal texture, color separation carried by topology and vertex-color-like shading.
Lighting/mood: warm indoor workyard light, cozy and practical, soft shadows.
Color palette: muted honey-brown oak, dark umber edges, tiny amounts of charcoal iron, deep soot-black shaft.

Avoid: freestanding portal, upright timber doorway, stone or brick pedestal, white masonry blocks, repeated decorative
bolts, orange varnish, giant ladder, railing, mine-shaft scale, flat black decal, modern trapdoor hardware, glossy PBR,
mobile-game polish, Minecraft, Roblox, photorealism, text, labels, UI, extra props, scenery, grass, dramatic perspective.
