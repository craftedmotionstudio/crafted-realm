# Workyard upstairs teaching hearth v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the many-sided hearth teaching family

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a purposeful medieval teaching-hearth family for a survival tutorial. Show a hand-built stone cooking hearth, a compact demonstration table, and two genuinely different learner stools. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin and a faint one-tile floor grid only for scale. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view.

The hearth must have an original broad arched silhouette made from irregular fitted stone voussoirs, a deep full-cavity soot backing, a thick stone hearth slab, an asymmetric oak mantel, and a visible slow cozy three-layer flame around two crossed logs. Add a forged pivoting cooking crane, short chain, round kettle, and a separate wall-side fireplace tool stand holding a shovel, poker, and brush. Every tool must touch its rack and remain readable without looking modern.

The demonstration table must not be round or rectangular: use a thick six-sided oak top with a shallow instructor-side notch, three splayed legs, triangular stretchers, and fitted shallow trays containing crossed kindling, a tinder curl, one flint, and one steel. The two stools must differ: one low three-legged splitter's stool and one narrow woven-seat learner stool, both player-scaled and angled toward the hearth.

Art direction: large readable polygon planes, hand-shaped custom silhouettes, controlled flat shading, cool grey-green stone, soot black, aged oak, dark forged iron, muted ochre rush, ember red, orange and warm yellow. Cozy and instructional rather than grand or dangerous. Materials and construction should be clear at Crafted Realm's elevated gameplay camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadow, generous padding, no room walls, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid a blocky rectangular fireplace, flat black void without depth, giant flames, fast or explosive fire, glossy metal, modern fireplace accessories, photorealism, dense tiny stones, plastic surfaces, floating tools, identical stools, generic dining furniture, Minecraft, and Roblox.
