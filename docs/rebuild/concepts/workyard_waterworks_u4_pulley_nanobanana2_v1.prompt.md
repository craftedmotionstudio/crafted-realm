# Workyard waterworks U4 pulley station v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the dock-edge bucket-pulley station (U4.02, U4.03)

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a small medieval bucket-pulley station standing at the edge of a wooden dock over calm dark pond water. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin standing beside it as the only scale reference. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view, framed close enough that the mechanism fills most of the image.

The station is a sturdy pegged timber frame: two upright posts with angled back braces bolted to the deck, a top crossbeam, and a short jib arm reaching out over the water with a small rope guide wheel at its tip. Between the posts sits a wooden winch drum on an iron axle, with a few visible wraps of hemp rope around the drum, and a hand crank with an offset arm and worn wooden grip on one side at a comfortable working height. From the jib guide a rope hangs straight down to an iron hook, which holds a small wooden stave bucket by its arched handle. The bucket has individually tapered staves, two dark iron hoops, an open top with real visible interior depth, and hangs just above a small cantilevered landing ledge fixed to the dock edge where it can rest. Below, the calm water waits for the bucket.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped silhouettes, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm aged oak, weathered deck planks, honey hemp rope, charcoal forged iron, muted teal-dark water. Construction and wear must come from topology and restrained face-color variation. Cozy, humble, medieval, workmanlike, slightly whimsical, and readable from an elevated game camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background beyond the water, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, photorealism, dense beveling, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, a floating unattached rope, a bucket fused to the deck, a solid black bucket opening, gears or modern pulleys, and crane-scale proportions.
