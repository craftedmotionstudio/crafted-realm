# Storm cellar ladder and descent — Nano Banana 2 prompt v2

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production reference for a custom-topology Blender traversal prop.
Create an original low-poly 3D concept sheet for a cozy medieval browser RPG, similar in broad art direction
to Old School RuneScape's readable, hand-shaped early-2000s low-poly charm. Do not copy any existing game's
ladder, hatch, composition, textures or protected asset data.

Show the exact same compact ladder-and-opening family from front three-quarter, true side and elevated
gameplay-camera views. Keep proportions identical across all three views.

The ladder:

- two crooked but structurally believable hand-hewn aged-oak rails;
- exactly seven individually shaped rungs with consistent human climbing spacing;
- approximately 0.6 units of clear width between rails;
- total rail height 2.3–2.45 units beside a 1.9-unit character;
- the ladder top must be only about one human head above the standing character, never giant-sized;
- simple forged-iron rail braces and visible wooden joinery;
- a natural shallow lean, not an extreme ramp.

The descent:

- one compact opening only 1 tile wide and about 1.1 tiles deep, shown on an unmistakable one-tile square grid;
- raised timber-and-masonry sill fitted tightly around the opening;
- visible inner wall thickness, dark descending shaft and two lower rung silhouettes;
- the hole must not consume multiple floor tiles or resemble a large mine shaft;
- include a short masonry/timber wall frame so the ladder has a believable support.

Scale is mandatory: a neutral 1.9-unit low-poly medieval character must stand immediately beside the ladder in
the front view, feet on the same floor plane. The character's head should reach roughly 78–83% of the ladder rail
height. Use warm flat-shaded aged oak, soot-dark iron and irregular gray-tan masonry. Neutral gray studio
background, soft shadows, no labels, no UI, no dramatic perspective and no photorealism.

Avoid: grass, a flat black decal, modern hardware, floating trapdoor, pristine machine-cut lumber, oversized
opening, giant ladder, extra props, ropes, railings, text, measurement labels or decorative scenery.
