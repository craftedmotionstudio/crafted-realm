# Workyard basement wall ladder — Nano Banana 2 corrective concept

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production reference for a custom-topology Blender architectural traversal prop.
Primary request: Design an original medieval wooden cellar ladder fitted against a rough stone basement wall and
leading into a dark ceiling opening. Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled
simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset, composition, texture, or protected data.

Show one consistent ladder installation in three views: elevated gameplay camera, front three-quarter construction
view, and true side view. It must look installed into the basement architecture, not pasted over a black rectangle.

Required design:
- two slightly crooked, thick aged-oak rails rising from the stone floor to a small dark ceiling/wall hatch;
- eight or nine plain hand-shaped wooden rungs, evenly spaced at believable human climbing intervals;
- rails about shoulder-width apart, total rise appropriate for a 3-unit cellar wall beside a 1.9-unit character;
- restrained joinery: rung ends visibly pass into the rails, with at most two small dark iron wall hooks near the top;
- a shallow natural lean against the wall, with both feet planted on shaped wooden pads on the floor;
- the top 15 percent of the rails disappear convincingly into a recessed timber-framed opening;
- the opening is narrow and architectural, with visible stone-wall depth and darkness beyond, not a black surface decal;
- irregular gray-brown fieldstone surrounds the opening and the existing wall remains visually dominant;
- large readable polygon planes, subtly uneven rail thickness and worn rung tops.

Style/medium: warm flat-shaded low-poly 3D, deliberately hand-modelled early-2000s fantasy-game charm, minimal texture,
shape-first construction, readable from an elevated gameplay camera.
Lighting/mood: cozy basement torchlight, amber wood against quiet gray stone, soft shadows without bloom.
Color palette: desaturated medium oak, darker worn rung tops, charcoal hooks, gray-tan stone, deep brown-black recess.

Avoid: bright orange wood, repeated bolts on every rung, metal rung straps, freestanding doorway or portal behind the
ladder, stone pedestal around its feet, giant proportions, wide mine opening, ladder floating off the wall, modern
hardware, glossy PBR, mobile-game polish, Minecraft, Roblox, photorealism, text, labels, UI, furniture, extra props.
