# Workyard exterior U3 chopping station v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the Survival Workyard chopping block, embedded axe, and sawbuck (U3.03, U3.04, U3.06)

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a medieval woodcutter's outdoor chopping station with two clearly separated objects. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin and a faint one-tile floor grid only as scale references. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view.

First object: a weathered knee-high chopping block — a broad irregular stump with a faceted bark rim, a slight root flare where it meets the ground, and a pale worn top scarred by several dark angled axe-cut marks. A full medieval felling axe is embedded in the block at a natural working angle: a shaped slightly curved wooden haft with a swollen knob end, and a hand-forged iron head whose flared cutting edge visibly bites into the wood with a small dark split at the contact. A couple of split wood chips lie at the base.

Second object: a simple sawbuck — two crossed X-frames of rough pegged beams joined by low stringers — cradling one round log that has been sawn partway through, with a visible dark saw kerf and a small pile of pale sawdust on the ground directly beneath the cut.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped silhouettes, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm aged oak, pale fresh-cut endgrain, darker weathered bark, charcoal forged iron. Construction and wear must come from topology and restrained face-color variation. Cozy, humble, medieval, workmanlike, slightly whimsical, and readable from an elevated game camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no room walls, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, photorealism, dense beveling, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, a plain smooth cylinder stump, a floating or painted-on axe, perfect symmetry, and oversized tools.
