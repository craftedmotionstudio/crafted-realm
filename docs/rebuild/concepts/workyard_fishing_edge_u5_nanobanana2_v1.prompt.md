# Workyard Fishing Edge U5 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: low-poly Blender reference board for a browser RPG world prop
Primary request: Create an original, cozy medieval small-net fishing station for the open edge of an existing timber pond dock. It must read immediately as a fishing spot from an elevated gameplay camera while remaining compact and believable.
Scene/backdrop: Show only the final two-tile-wide end of a weathered L-shaped timber dock and the fishable water immediately beside it. Include a faint one-tile grid and one neutral 1.85-tile adventurer mannequin for scale. The dock itself is context, not a redesign target.
Subject: A subtle rectangular patch of clear blue-green pond water beside the open dock edge, three small faceted fish silhouettes visible below the surface, two sparse ring ripples and a few bubbles, plus one hand-woven open wicker creel sitting at the outer side of the deck. The creel must have a clearly modeled rim, woven body, hinged lid resting partly open, shoulder strap, and a visible warm-brown interior—never a black void. Keep the center of the dock clear for the player. Include one small knotted net-weight detail attached to the creel, but no fishing rod.
Style/medium: Original warm flat-shaded low-poly 3D concept, hand-authored 2007-era browser RPG readability, similar in visual simplicity and gameplay-camera clarity to Old School RuneScape without copying any RuneScape asset or layout. Deliberately faceted fish and wicker, controlled asymmetry, fitted contacts, restrained material count.
Composition/framing: Elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay view. The dock approaches from the left, the water lies to the right, the player stands one tile back from the open edge, and the creel sits off to one side without blocking the cardinal lane. Also include three small inset motion thumbnails labeled visually by pose only: calm fish drift, bite ripple, catch lift. No written text is required.
Lighting/mood: Warm late-morning light, calm water, cozy and inviting rather than glossy or cinematic.
Color palette: Weathered honey-brown timber, muted hemp and wicker, blue-green water, pale silver-blue fish with ochre fins, restrained cream bubbles.
Materials/textures: Flat colors with deliberate face-to-face tone variation; no photoreal textures, no shiny plastic, no noisy surface maps.
Constraints: Preserve a 1.2-tile clear walking lane; keep all fish and ripple geometry below or flush with the water; make the creel player-scale; show real interior depth; make every attachment contact physically believable; no modern objects; no giant marker; no random crates; no signs; no character animation; no fishing rod; no opaque black holes; no watermark.
Avoid: Minecraft blocks, Roblox plastic, hyper-polished mobile-game art, dense clutter, oversized props, floating fish above the water, perfectly smooth cylinders, copied OSRS assets.
