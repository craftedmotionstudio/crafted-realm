# Workyard upstairs story fixtures and textiles v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the lodge's wall lessons and woven route markers

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept board showing six clearly separated medieval survival-lodge fixtures: a framed firemaking lesson board, a carved storm-tally beam, a wall-mounted fishing-net drying rack, a small relief picture called The Storm Warden, a long lodge runner rug, and a compact many-sided hearth rug. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view. Include a faint one-tile grid and one neutral 1.85-tile adventurer mannequin only as scale references.

The firemaking board is an irregular dark oak frame around a green-black slate, with thick pale chalk topology showing a dry base, crossed kindling, wind arrow and safe stone ring—no written words. The tally is a thick crooked oak lintel with eleven deep ivory-filled cuts and one diagonal cross-cut. The net rack has two shaped wall brackets, a drying rail, a sagging diamond-knotted rope net, cork floats and three stone weights; everything is attached and oriented for a wall.

The Storm Warden picture is a small chunky relief artwork, not a photographic texture: an original hooded medieval island keeper carrying a lantern against an orange storm sky, assembled from large pixel-like colored relief tiles inside a dark oak frame. Keep it below player height when mounted.

The lodge runner uses a muted deep-blue field, ochre border, moss-green diamond path markers and short cream fringe. The hearth rug is a related but distinct eight-sided design with a madder-red field, ochre ring and one charcoal sunburst. Both should have slightly irregular woven edges, visible large stitches, real thickness, and restrained color—never a perfectly flat colored plane.

Art direction: handmade, practical, cozy, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm dark oak, chalk, rope, muted blue, ochre, moss, madder and cream. Every object must have a clear lesson, route, or story purpose and read at an elevated gameplay camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no room walls, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid modern signage, legible writing, smooth digital screens, photorealistic paintings, glossy fabric, Persian luxury ornament, paper-thin rugs, floating net parts, random decoration, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, and dense texture noise.
