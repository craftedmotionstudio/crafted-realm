# Workyard waterworks U4 dock v1 — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

**Tool:** Gemini 3 Pro Image / Nano Banana 2
**Use case:** stylized-concept
**Purpose:** original concept reference for the Survival Workyard L-shaped pond dock (U4.01)

## Final prompt

Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.

Create a clean low-poly 3D concept render of a small medieval L-shaped wooden pond dock. Include a simple neutral 1.85-tile-tall low-poly adventurer mannequin standing on the deck and a faint one-tile grid on the visible shore only as scale references. Use an elevated three-quarter orthographic gameplay-camera view over calm dark pond water.

The dock leaves a grassy stone-edged shore, runs straight out over the water about four tiles, turns right at a slightly wider corner platform, and runs another four tiles to land on the adjacent bank — an unmistakable L silhouette, two tiles wide, with a clear walking lane down the middle. The deck is made of individually cut planks with slight width and tone variation, small honest gaps, and a couple of newer repaired boards. Round timber piles with weathered proud tops support the deck at the edges, driven visibly down into the water, with angled bracing beams below deck level. A low rope-and-post rail runs along parts of the outer edges only — the shore entry, the bank landing, one long open fishing edge, and the corner work area stay completely open. Stone footings and packed earth meet the deck at both shore contacts. On the corner platform stands a simple timber bucket-pulley frame, seen at distance.

Art direction: deliberately hand-shaped silhouettes, large readable polygon planes, flat or vertex-color-like shading, warm aged oak, sun-bleached weathered planks, darker wet pile bases, honey hemp rope, charcoal iron pegs, muted teal-dark water. Construction and wear must come from topology and restrained face-color variation. Cozy, humble, medieval, workmanlike, slightly whimsical, and readable from an elevated game camera.

Presentation: plain warm charcoal studio background beyond the water, soft grounded shadows, generous padding, no labels, no text, no watermark.

Avoid glossy mobile-game rendering, photorealism, dense beveling, Minecraft blocks, Roblox proportions, toy plastic, perfectly identical planks, a plain rectangular pier without the L turn, railings blocking the walkway ends, floating piles, and modern marina hardware.
