# Worn Gear Starter Family — Nano Banana 2 concept prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: low-poly Blender reference board for a browser RPG's first-hour held equipment
Primary request: Create an original board of the six starter tools and weapons a new adventurer holds in their hand during the first hour: a bronze hatchet, a bronze pickaxe, a bronze sword, a bronze dagger, a worn wooden shortbow, and a round wooden shield. Similar to Old School RuneScape's 2007-era low-poly art style and hand-modelled simplicity, while remaining an original design and not copying any exact asset.
Scene/backdrop: A plain warm workshop wall and bench display: the six items arranged in one orderly row, each standing or hanging clearly separated, over a faint one-tile grid, with one neutral 1.85-tile adventurer mannequin at the left end for scale. No other clutter.
Subject: Each item reads as hand-forged and hand-carved. The hatchet: a forged bronze head with visible poll, cheeks, eye and a brighter flared bit edge, seated on a subtly curved oak haft with a swollen end knob and a small leather collar. The pickaxe: one crescent forged bronze head with two tapering points and brighter worn tips, through a stout curved haft. The sword: a straight bronze blade with a visible central fuller, brighter ground edges, a drooped flared crossguard, leather-wrapped grip in distinct wrap bands, and a faceted pommel. The dagger: a short leaf-shaped bronze blade, small straight guard, wrapped grip, disc pommel. The shortbow: a visibly worn single-stave bow with tapering faceted limbs, string nocks, a leather grip wrap and one off-center repair wrap band. The shield: a round shield of five visible vertical planks in slightly different wood tones, a riveted iron rim, a faceted iron center boss, and honest edge wear.
Style/medium: Original warm flat-shaded low-poly 3D concept, hand-authored 2007-era browser RPG readability. Deliberately faceted forged metal, controlled asymmetry, fitted joints and collars, restrained material count. Large readable polygon planes; construction and wear come from topology and color placement, not surface texture.
Composition/framing: Straight-on slightly elevated orthographic view of the display row so every silhouette is fully readable; each item roughly its true relative size against the mannequin (hatchet and pickaxe about one third of the mannequin's height, sword just over half, dagger about one quarter, bow about half, shield about one third wide).
Lighting/mood: Warm late-morning workshop light, cozy and inviting rather than glossy or cinematic.
Color palette: Muted forge bronze with brighter honed edges, warm oak and dark walnut hafts, chestnut leather wraps, pale hemp string, weathered plank browns, dull dark iron rim and boss.
Materials/textures: Flat colors with deliberate face-to-face tone variation; no photoreal textures, no shiny plastic, no noisy surface maps.
Constraints: Exactly six items plus the mannequin; every item complete and fully visible; grips and handles clearly sized for the mannequin's hand; no duplicate designs; no runes or glow; no modern objects; no text labels; no watermark.
Avoid: Minecraft blocks, Roblox plastic, hyper-polished mobile-game art, dense clutter, oversized fantasy proportions, perfectly smooth cylinders, copied OSRS assets.
