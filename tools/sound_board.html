Crafted Realm sound board

Every sound effect in the game, synthesized (src/sfx_lib.js, recipes in src/sfx_recipes.js). Play is the new sound, Play x3 plays it three times as the game would (each play is nudged a little in pitch and level), and Old plays the sound it replaced, where there was one. Inventory and wiring: docs/rebuild/HOLM_SOUND_INVENTORY.md.
